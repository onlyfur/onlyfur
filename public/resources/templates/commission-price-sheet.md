## FAQ

### How do I commission you?
Send me a message with your idea, including character references and any specific details about what you want.

### Do you offer payment plans?
For commissions over $200, I can split payment into 3 installments: 50% upfront, 25% at sketch approval, 25% on completion.

### Can I get a discount for multiple pieces?
Yes! Orders of 3+ pieces receive a 10% discount on the total.

### What if I don't like the final result?
I provide sketches and progress updates to ensure you're happy throughout the process. Major concerns should be addressed during the sketch phase.

### Do you draw NSFW content?
Yes, but only for clients who can verify they are 18+. NSFW commissions have an additional $25 fee.

### Can I use the art commercially?
Only if you purchase commercial rights (+100% base price). Standard commissions are for personal use only.

### How do I pay?
I send PayPal invoices for professional transactions. Payment must be completed before I begin work.

### Can you rush my commission?
Rush orders are available for +50% of the base price and will be completed within one week.
