# OnlyFur Creator Onboarding Guide

## Welcome to OnlyFur! 🐾

This comprehensive guide will help you set up your creator account and start building your furry content business.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Account Creation
- Sign up with a valid email address
- Choose a memorable username (this cannot be changed later)
- Complete email verification
- Set up two-factor authentication for security

### Step 2: Profile Setup
- Upload a high-quality profile picture
- Create an engaging banner image (1500x500px recommended)
- Write a compelling bio that describes your content style
- Set your location and interests
- Add social media links

### Step 3: Content Categories
Choose your primary content categories:
- **Fursuit Content** - Costume photography and videos
- **Digital Art** - Illustrations, commissions, and artwork
- **Photography** - Character photos and scenes
- **Stories** - Written content and narratives
- **Live Streams** - Real-time interaction with fans

### Step 4: Subscription Tiers
Set up your subscription levels:
- **Basic Tier ($9.99/month)** - Access to general content
- **Premium Tier ($19.99/month)** - Exclusive content and early access
- **VIP Tier ($39.99/month)** - Personal interaction and custom content

### Step 5: Content Planning
- Plan your first 10 posts before going live
- Create a content calendar
- Prepare welcome messages for new subscribers
- Set up custom pricing for commissions

## Best Practices

### Profile Optimization
- Use keywords in your bio related to your content
- Update your profile regularly with new achievements
- Respond to comments and messages promptly
- Cross-promote on other social platforms

### Content Strategy
- Post consistently (aim for 3-5 times per week)
- Vary your content types to keep followers engaged
- Use relevant tags to improve discoverability
- Engage with other creators in your niche

### Building Your Community
- Welcome new subscribers personally
- Ask for feedback and suggestions
- Host regular Q&A sessions
- Collaborate with other furry creators

## Safety and Guidelines

### Content Guidelines
- All content must comply with platform rules
- Respect copyright and intellectual property
- No harassment or discriminatory content
- Maintain appropriate content warnings

### Personal Safety
- Never share personal information publicly
- Use watermarks on your original artwork
- Report any inappropriate behavior immediately
- Keep business and personal accounts separate

## Monetization Tips

### Pricing Your Content
- Research similar creators' pricing
- Start competitively and increase as you grow
- Offer limited-time promotions for new subscribers
- Create bundle deals for multiple services

### Growing Your Income
- Offer custom commissions
- Sell merchandise featuring your characters
- Provide exclusive behind-the-scenes content
- Host paid virtual events or workshops
